Purchase Order Entry Process

Purpose

Creating a purchase order for materials and services in SAP Business One.

Procedure

Step 1

Open Purchase Order Creation Window

Open the Purchase Order creation window from the SAP menu.

Step 2

Enter Document Overview Details

Fill in the document overview section with relevant details such as vendor, document date, purchase order number, and delivery address.

Step 3

Save Purchase Order

Click on the 'Save' button to save the initial draft of the purchase order.

Step 4

Add Material Details

Enter the material details including item number, quantity, unit price, and other relevant information.

Step 5

Save and Confirm Purchase Order

Click on 'Save' again to save the material details. Then click on 'Confirm' to finalize the purchase order.

Step 6

Review Document Overview

Review and confirm that all fields are correctly filled in, especially checking vendor details, document date, and purchase order number.

Step 7

Enter Vendor Number

Manually enter the supplier's number if it is not automatically populated.

Step 8

Save Purchase Order Again

Click on 'Save' to save any changes made in the vendor details section.

Step 9

Review and Confirm Document

Check all sections of the purchase order for accuracy, including vendor information, delivery date, and quantity.

Step 10

Enter Purchase Organization

Manually enter or select the purchasing organization if it is not automatically filled in.

Step 11

Select Purchase Group and Company Code

Ensure that the purchase group and company code are correctly selected or entered, if they are not pre-filled.

Step 12

Enter Material Details Manually

If necessary, enter material details manually since some fields might be empty or auto-populated incorrectly.

Step 13

Check and Fill in Document Information

Verify that all document information is correctly filled, including delivery date, purchase order number, and plant details.

Step 14

Review Purchase Order Details

Ensure the purchase order details are accurate by reviewing all sections of the document overview.

Step 15

Manually Enter Missing Information

Enter any missing information manually if it is not automatically populated.

Step 16

Check Document Overview Again

Review the document overview to ensure all fields are correctly filled in and save any changes made.

Step 17

Add Material or Service Details

Enter material or service details including item number, quantity, unit price, etc., to the purchase order.

Step 18

Enter Material Number

In the material field, enter the material number for the item being purchased.
