## Procedure

### Step 1
**Initial Purchase Order Setup**
Navigate to the purchase order editing screen and select standard PO.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00000.png)

### Step 2
**Document Overview**
Review document overview including vendor, document date, purchasing organization, and company code. Ensure the correct values are set or enter manually if necessary.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00001.png)

### Step 3
**Purchasing Organization and Group**
Enter the purchasing organization and group, and company code. These may be pre-filled based on your configuration.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00002.png)

### Step 4
**Document Details**
Provide vendor number, document date, purchase order quantity, delivery date, and other relevant details. Ensure the correct values are entered or use default settings if applicable.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00003.png)

### Step 5
**Conditions and Texts**
Enter conditions and texts for the purchase order, such as delivery terms, pricing, and group parameters. Ensure all fields are correctly filled.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00004.png)

### Step 6
**Save and Continue**
Save the document and move to the next section for adding materials or services.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00005.png)

### Step 7
**Adding Materials/Services**
Enter material numbers, quantities, and other relevant details. Ensure all required fields are filled correctly.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00006.png)

### Step 8
**Review and Finalize**
Review the entire purchase order document for accuracy and completeness before finalizing it.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00008.png)

### Step 9
**Save as Template (Optional)**
Optionally save the completed purchase order as a template for future use.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00009.png)

### Step 10
**Check Messages and Confirmations**
Review any messages or confirmations related to the document setup. Ensure all steps are completed successfully.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00010.png)

### Step 11
**Document Overview Revisited**
Review and update the document overview if necessary, ensuring all fields are correctly filled.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00012.png)

### Step 12
**Item Overview Section**
Move to the item overview section where you can add materials or services for purchase. Ensure correct material numbers and quantities are entered.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00013.png)

### Step 13
**Document Details (Revisited)**
Review the document details again, ensuring all fields are correctly filled for each item. Enter delivery date if not pre-filled.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00014.png)

### Step 14
**Document Overview (Revisited)**
Review the document overview again, ensuring all fields are correctly filled for each item. Ensure correct delivery date is entered.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00015.png)

### Step 15
**Document Details (Revisited)**
Review and update the document details if necessary, ensuring all fields are correctly filled for each item. Ensure correct delivery date is entered.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00017.png)

### Step 16
**Document Details (Revisited)**
Review the document details again, ensuring all fields are correctly filled for each item. Ensure correct delivery date is entered.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00018.png)

### Step 17
**Item Overview Section**
Move to the item overview section where you can add materials or services for purchase. Ensure correct material numbers and quantities are entered.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00019.png)

### Step 18
**Enter Material Number**
In the material field, enter the material number to be purchased.
![Screenshot](../frames/frame_00020.png)
